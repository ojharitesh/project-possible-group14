## Part 2: Ask & Uncover Pain Points — Brainstorming Log

- Mutual friends
- Making the first connection
- Getting over the fear of rejection or eliminating awkward situations
- Feeling judged by the other person/party
- Embarrassed to share experiences
- Finding a common interest with new people gets harder as you get old.

**Primary Rough Problem Selected to Pursue:**

> Building connection with common interest/connection, through small talk and working on how to feel more confident.

Questions the group used/asked:

- When you try to accomplish this, what steps feel the most cumbersome or require the most effort?
- At what point in the process do you usually find yourself slowing down or needing to work around an obstacle?
- How much time or effort does this routine typically take out of your day or week?
- What manual workarounds or extra steps do you rely on to get through this process?
- What parts of this experience feel opaque, unclear, or hard to make sense of?
- When you encounter instructions or steps here, what usually causes hesitation or second-guessing?
- Can you describe a recent moment when dealing with this situation caused real annoyance or anxiety?
- At what point in this experience do people most commonly feel tempted to give up or abandon the process?

## Part 3: Frame Your Problem

### Problem Statement

> When meeting new people, it is hard to find common ground, having fear of judgement and embarrassment, which results in a lot of missed opportunities.

### Jobs to Be Done

1. Starting a conversation
2. Feeling confident to keep it going
3. Quickly identify common interest
4. Building lasting connections

### Objective

> Help students to identify common grounds fast, maintain conversation, and connect with others outside the classroom.
