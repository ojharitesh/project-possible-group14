## Part 2: Ask & Uncover Pain Points

Prompts used by the group covered Difficult, Inefficient, Confusing, and Frustrating experiences.

### Brainstorming Log

- Mutual friends
- Making the first connection
- Getting over the fear of rejection or eliminating awkward situations
- Feeling judged by the other person/party
- Embarrassed to share experiences
- Finding a common interest with new people gets harder as you get old.

### Primary Rough Problem Selected to Pursue

> Students can have difficulty building new social connections because they struggle to start conversations, identify common interests, and feel confident enough to continue interacting with someone they do not know well.

## Part 3: Frame Your Problem

### Problem Statement

> When students meet new people, they can struggle to identify common ground and start or maintain conversations because of fear of judgment, rejection, or embarrassment. This can cause them to avoid interactions or miss opportunities to build meaningful social connections.

### Jobs to Be Done

1. Starting a conversation
2. Feeling confident to keep it going
3. Quickly identify common interest
4. Building lasting connections

### Objective

> Help students to identify common grounds fast, maintain conversation, and connect with others outside the classroom.

## Part 4: Looking Ahead — Early User Interview Questions (The Mom Test)

1. How often do you run into this problem?
2. What are times that you quickly identify common interests, what did you do to make that happen? Can that be transferred to other scenarios?
3. What would you consider to be an obstacle, what's preventing you from overcoming them?
4. What are some of the solutions that come into your mind? Is it effective?
5. "What made that experience awkward or uncomfortable?"
6. "What made you decide whether to keep talking or stop?"
7. "Was there anything that made you feel more confident?"

Template prompts referenced: Past Incident, Current Workaround, Consequence, Frequency, Past Attempts.
