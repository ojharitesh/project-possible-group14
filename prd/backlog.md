# WarmUp backlog

Ideas for after v1. v1 covers only "Quickly identify common interest" (see `implementation-plan/plan.md`).

## Other jobs, for later versions

- **Starting a conversation:** more openers, and openers matched to the setting
- **Feeling confident to keep it going:** prompts during the conversation, "keep it going" tips
- **Building lasting connections:** optional contact exchange, follow-up nudges

## Ideas and features

- An organizer flow: create an event and start the activity
- Real live matching across devices, plus timed rounds and rotation
- Sharing interests before the event (solution option 1)
- An after-the-conversation helper (solution option 5)
- A results view for organizers that counts shared-interest "yes" answers and average confidence change
- Expanding to classes and other group settings
- Publishing to a shareable link

## Research and open questions (from the PRD)

- Run user interviews with students who attend club events, and with club leaders or hosts
- Would students actually join a structured, organizer-led activity?
- What confidence improvement counts as success (the threshold)?
